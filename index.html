About Me

I'm a Research Scientist at NVIDIA, where I've studied LLM inference accelerators, vector quantization, sparsity, and most recently, AI-for-hardware-design. Previously, I was a PhD student at UC Berkeley, where I was advised by Krste Asanović, and also collaborated very closely with Sophia Shao. Over there, I led the development of Gemmini, a platform for full-system, full-stack evaluation of DNN accelerator workloads. Before that, I was an undergrad at UT Austin, where I helped develop MAVBench, a benchmarking platform for drone processors, with Vijay Janapa Reddi.

My research interests include the design of hardware accelerators for DNN workloads, and agile hardware design methodologies. I've designed and contributed to hardware DSLs in the past, and my hope is that AI will not make innovation of that sort obselete :).
